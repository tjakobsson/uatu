Static reference only. The runnable prototype and temporary services have been removed. These screenshots preserve the reviewed visual design. The production scope and architecture are defined by the OpenSpec proposal and design document, not by the prototype's implementation.

Preview now has persistent translucent Back to Files, Previous, and Next controls. They step through files in the same directory, without reloading the workspace. The controls default to the bottom left, rise above the expanded workspace selector, and remain visible after it collapses.

Review fixes: history preserves the opened workspace independently of management actions; defaults start from current values; clone unlocking retains the form; navigation text scales; focused controls are not timed out; Keep open is available; Terminal output badges are retained; and file-index failures show an explicit Retry.

Image sequence: the captures show Previous/Next through sibling images. At the ends, the unavailable direction is very light gray and disabled. A single-file directory hides both arrows. Failures show a separate message and Retry, not a false boundary.

Placement: Hub Settings → Preview File Controls → Side. Choose Left or Right; the preference is remembered on this device. The controls do not appear in Files, Chat, or Terminal.

Motion: a restrained, in-place materialize-style fade replaces the genie effect. The bar has no travel, stretching, scaling, or bounce. It fades in over 180 ms and out over 280 ms; these are prototype timings, not Apple-prescribed values. The seven-second idle delay is unchanged, and controls work during the reveal.

Apple documents materialize for appearing glass effects without geometry matching, including controls too far apart to morph together. This web approximation follows that intent rather than reproducing the native renderer. Reduce Motion skips the animation.

Terminal visibility: only the overlaid selector and arrow change palette in Terminal. Deep blue glass, bright labels, a pale selected-state pill, and a cyan edge remain readable against the dark terminal. The terminal's styling, controls, and contents are not changed.

Return navigation: a fresh Hub shows only Hub and Settings. Open a workspace and return: the bar then reads Return to [workspace], Hub, Settings. Return to stays available while browsing Settings and its detail screens. The workspace slides right to reveal the Hub underneath, restoring the Hub's previous scroll position.

Position: drag up or down either edge, or across to the other side. The position is shared across workspaces and synchronized between tabs on this Hub, on this device. Reloads retain it; other Hub origins have their own preference. The expanded selector always stays at the bottom. Arrow keys reposition the handle; Home restores its starting position.

The prototype captures used main's frontend at commit b697458f08b1 with fictional data. Its iframe, fake services, synthetic navigation adapter, and retained-client mechanics are not part of the production proposal. Production will preserve main's existing routing and session lifecycles.
